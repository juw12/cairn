# Design principles

A field notebook, not a dashboard. Full reasoning in `plan/DESIGN.md`.

- Calm by default: no red badges, no infinite feed
- Progress you can see: the cairn grows a stone per finished task
- Evidence over claims: [[0003-no-vanity-metrics]]
- One signal colour, used only where attention matters

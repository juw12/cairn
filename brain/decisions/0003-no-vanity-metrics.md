## Context

Follower counts and likes reward performing over doing.

## Options

1. Show followers, stars, likes
2. Show only evidence of work

## Decision

Profiles show what people are working on, shipped and decided. No follower counts, no streak guilt, no red badges.

## Consequences

Less addictive, more honest. Growth has to come from usefulness.
